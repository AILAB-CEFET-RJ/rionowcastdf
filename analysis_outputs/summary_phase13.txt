AVISOS / NOTAS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- WARNING: Regime occupancy is conditioned on Phase 6 timestamps with radar availability. Missingness may not be random with respect to weather.
- WARNING: The reference number of regimes is fixed by configuration; candidate k diagnostics are sensitivity information, not an automatic proof of a uniquely correct number of meteorological regimes.
- WARNING: Absolute-space regimes can reflect seasonality and the diurnal cycle. The anomaly_month_hour space is provided to isolate departures from month x local-hour climatology.
- WARNING: Regimes are defined from ERA5 predictors only. Radar metrics are joined after clustering to avoid direct target leakage.
- WARNING: The 12 raw channels define clustering; derived variables characterize regimes but do not influence cluster geometry, reducing redundancy.
- WARNING: KMeans imposes approximately convex clusters in PCA space. Meteorological states need not be truly discrete or spherical.
- WARNING: One-hour transitions use only exact consecutive UTC timestamp pairs; gaps are not bridged.
- WARNING: Regime labels R1..Rk are display identifiers ordered by mean tcwv then t2m. They are not ordinal physical rankings.
- WARNING: Phase 13 is descriptive. Phase 14 remains responsible for formal distribution-shift analysis, especially the 2023-2024 degradation.
- NOTE: absolute space answers which broad atmospheric states recur over the historical record.
- NOTE: anomaly_month_hour space answers which synoptic/anomalous states recur after removing the mean seasonal-diurnal background.
- NOTE: PCA is fit separately in each regime space on standardized raw predictors and retains the configured cumulative variance.
- NOTE: Cluster stability is summarized with Adjusted Rand Index across multiple KMeans seeds for the reference k.
- NOTE: Event enrichment is expressed relative to the global event rate among the same radar-available timestamps.
- NOTE: Yearly regime prevalence and regime-specific event rates are screening outputs for Phase 14, not a formal shift test.
- NOTE: All associations between regime and radar are descriptive and do not establish causality.
